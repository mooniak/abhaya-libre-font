About these pages

Mooniak designs and develops typefaces for Sinhala, Tamil and Latin scripts, many of them open source. Each font lives in its own repository; every time its dev branch changes, the font is rebuilt and tested automatically, and the result is published here so designers and testers can see where it stands.

Each build is checked for quality (fontspector) and measured against a design-stage model: stage 0–10, from the first drawn letters to the full conjunct and long-tail character set. The proof pages only show the text a font has earned by its stage, so an early font is not judged on glyphs it does not have yet.
